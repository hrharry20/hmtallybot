HM Tally Bot

Privacy Policy · HS Software Solutions

Effective date: 29 October 2026 (updated 29 October 2026: Accessibility features)

HM Tally Bot ("the app") is published by HS Software Solutions (Harbansh Singh), India. It shows the Tally accounting data of your own companies on your phone — ledgers, outstanding, balance sheet, profit & loss, stock and vouchers — and can answer your parties on WhatsApp with their statements and bills. This policy explains what the app uses, why, and your choices.

In short: your accounting data stays in your own Google Drive and on your phone. We, the developer, never receive it. We only keep your name, email and trial dates to manage your licence.

2. Data that stays on your device or in your Google account

  • Your company data (ledgers, vouchers, stock, balances, party mobile numbers): read from the Google Sheets that the HM Tally / Web ERP desktop software keeps in the TallyERPWeb folder of your Google Drive, and cached on your phone so reports open instantly and work offline. The app uses the limited drive.file permission, so it can only see files created by our software or opened with it — not the rest of your Drive. If you edit a master (for example a party's mobile number) in the app, that change is written back to your own sheet.
  • WhatsApp messages (notification access, optional): when you turn on the WhatsApp auto-reply bot, the app reads incoming WhatsApp notifications on your phone to recognise requests such as "statement" or "outstanding" from your saved parties and reply to them. Messages are processed only on the phone; a short activity log (who asked what) is kept on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Contacts (optional): used on the phone only, to match a WhatsApp sender shown by name to the mobile number on their ledger. Contacts are never uploaded.
  • Accessibility service (optional, "Auto-send"): see section 4.
  • PDF statements and bills are generated on the phone. When your phone is locked and a PDF cannot be attached in WhatsApp, the app uploads that PDF to a folder named HM Tally Bot shared in your Google Drive, sets it to "anyone with the link can view" and sends the link to the party who asked. You can delete these files from your Drive at any time.

3. How data is shared

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Statements, bills and messages are sent only to the WhatsApp contacts of your own parties, as a result of your settings or your taps. Data moves between the app and Google over encrypted HTTPS connections. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Sign-In, Drive and Sheets.

4. Accessibility Service disclosure

HM Tally Bot uses Android's AccessibilityService API for optional WhatsApp features:

  • Auto-send — only right after the app itself has opened a WhatsApp chat to send a statement, bill or message you asked for (or that your auto-reply bot prepared), it checks that the chat on screen is the intended party and presses WhatsApp's Send button for you.
  • Reply in the open chat — WhatsApp shows no notification for the chat you have open, so the bot reads the newest message that arrives at the bottom of that chat, and the chat's name, to answer it the same way it answers notifications.
  • Bubble — it notes only which app is in front, so the floating share bubble appears over WhatsApp and hides in other apps.

It does not read older messages, other chats or the screens of other apps, does not record your screen, and sends nothing to us or anyone else; everything is processed on the phone. The feature is off by default: the app explains it and you switch it on yourself in Android Settings → Accessibility, and you can switch it off at any time. Everything else in the app works without it (you then press Send yourself).

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete your account inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your name, photo and Google ID from our register, revokes the app's Google access and deletes every company copy cached on the phone.
  • Or request deletion on the web: Delete account page. We act within 7 days.
  • We keep only your email address and trial dates for up to 3 years after deletion, solely to prevent the free trial being restarted.
  • Your Tally company sheets in your own Google Drive are yours; the app never deletes them. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the phone.
  • You can also remove the app's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
